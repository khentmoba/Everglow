# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Clair is the primary user. She opens Everglow on her phone, most often at
night in a dim room, and sometimes on a tablet. Khent builds it with her
and uses it too. Family guests (Breyan, Octagram) use only the movie side.

## Product Purpose

A private web app for one couple, Khent and Clair: a shared home for their
letters, chat, photos, journal, plans, games, money and media. Success is
Clair feeling that the app is obviously theirs, warm, and simple to use.

## Positioning

It is not a product for many couples. Every screen is written for these two
people, their dates, their words and their history.

## Operating Context

Phone first (about 430px wide), tablet second. Installed as a home screen
web app. Opened in short visits: check the home screen, read a note, add a
memory, pick tonight's plan. The movie and media sections (Cinema, Anime,
Books, Mangacelestia) are their own niche worlds and keep their own looks.

## Capabilities and Constraints

React + Vite app in `react/`, Firebase backend. Colors and type come from
`react/src/core/theme/`; shared UI from `react/src/shared/widgets/`.
Couple-only data stays private. Demo screenshots use agent-mode fake data.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Everglow. Mascot: the Motchi cat.
- Night-time mood and rose as the love colour (confirmed by Khent, 2026-11-18).
- Voice: plain, warm, lower-case-friendly, never corporate.

## Evidence on Hand

Real content lives in Firestore and is never used in screenshots. Agent mode
supplies fake demo content for every couple screen.

## Product Principles

1. Clair first: obvious beats clever.
2. Small, simple systems over impressive machinery.
3. Every screen should feel like part of one home, not twenty apps.
4. Private by default.
